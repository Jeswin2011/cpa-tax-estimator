What it does

Built for sole proprietors, single-member LLCs, C-corporations and the CPAs who work with them.

Reads your files

CSV, Excel and OFX/QFX bank downloads open instantly. PDFs, scans and phone photos of receipts are read by AI.

Sorts every transaction

Rent, wages, utilities, meals (50%), travel and more, matching Schedule C. Transfers and owner draws are left out.

Estimates the tax

Self-employment tax, the 20% QBI deduction and the 2025 brackets, or the flat 21% for C-corps. Shows the balance due or refund.

You stay in control

Click any category to see its purchases, and move anything that's in the wrong place. The totals update instantly.

Catches AI mistakes

Statements must reconcile to their opening and closing balance. Blurry lines are flagged, and duplicate receipts are unticked.

Private by design

Your numbers stay on your computer. The AI key is encrypted by your operating system.

How it works

Three steps, a few minutes.

Upload

Drop in a year of bank and card statements, receipts or payroll reports.

Review

Check the categories and fix anything with one click. AI-read documents show the original side by side.

Estimate

See profit, tax owed, what you've already paid, and your quarterly estimated payment.

Every purchase, grouped and editable

Each category is a box showing its total and number of transactions. Open one to see every purchase, and use Move to if something belongs elsewhere.

Install

Download the installer above, or install from a terminal with one command:

irm https://jeswin2011.github.io/cpa-tax-estimator/install.ps1 | iex
curl -fsSL https://jeswin2011.github.io/cpa-tax-estimator/install.sh | sh

The app isn't code-signed yet, so on first launch Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (click More info → Run anyway). On a Mac, if you downloaded the .dmg in a browser, right-click the app and choose Open. The terminal installers avoid both prompts.
